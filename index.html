JRG-01 Linear

A quiet study in mechanical harmony.

Developed over eighteen months of iterative prototyping, the JRG-01 eliminates stem wobble through precision injection-molded tooling. The factory light dry-film lubrication preserves the natural friction coefficient of polished POM against smoky polycarbonate without sluggishness or viscosity drift.

JARIGASME SIGNATURE

A switch should feel right.

Mechanical switches are intimate tools. We tuned three foundational parameters to ensure JARIGASME neutrality and natural feedback.

PARAMETER 01POLISHED POM

Frictionless Glide

Precision-tooled POM slider rails provide instant glide without the stick-slip effect typical of unpolished resins. Movement remains smooth from first press to millionth cycle.

PARAMETER 0262GF PROGRESSIVE

Controlled Bottom-Out

The 62gf progressive bottom-out cushions your stroke before harsh bottom impact, reducing finger strain during prolonged typing sessions without sacrificing crisp actuation.

PARAMETER 03SMOKY POLYCARBONATE

Acoustic Attenuation

The smoky polycarbonate upper housing delivers a clean, focused clack with minimal high-frequency housing chatter. Muffled on the return, resonant on the downstroke.
